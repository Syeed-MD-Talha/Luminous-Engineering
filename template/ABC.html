High-Quality Nippon Paints For Your Home

  • 1. Odour-less Medifresh
  • 2. Odour-less Easy Wash
  • 3. Odour-less All In 1
  • 4. Easy Wash with Teflon
  • 5. 3 in 1 Medifresh
  • 6. Vinilex 5000
  • 7. Matex


Odour-less Medifresh, used by our contractors, is ideal for those sensitive to strong smells. It emits minimal odour during application, ensuring comfort. Washable and stain-resistant, it withstands regular cleaning without losing quality or colour. Its low sheen finish adds a subtle shine to your walls, keeping them bright and sophisticated.

Odour-less Easy Wash paint is preferred for home painting services by individuals who are sensitive to smells as it minimises odour emissions, and is easy to clean. Like Odour-less Medifresh, it offers effortless wall maintenance while providing a low-sheen finish that enhances brightness and depth in your space.

Odour-less All-In-1 paint is selected by contractors for smell-sensitive individuals as it emits minimal odour during painting, prioritising your comfort. Its washable formula allows easy wall and ceiling cleaning while maintaining durability. With a matte finish, it adds depth and character to your living spaces.

Easy Wash with Teflon paint is designed to be washable, allowing for regular cleaning of walls and ceilings to maintain a clean and fresh appearance. It withstands cleaning without fading or losing quality. Its low sheen finish adds a subtle shine, brightening your space. Teflon Technology enhances durability by repelling stains and dirt, keeping your walls looking new for longer.

3 in 1 Medifresh paint offers an antibacterial feature and is preferred by our professional painters for residential customers who prioritise a hygienic environment. It is especially suitable for those with conditions such as asthma, flu, and diarrhoea. Additionally, the paint has a low sheen finish, adding a subtle shine to the walls.

Vinilex 5000 paint provides a timeless, classic matte finish that gives walls a sophisticated and elegant look. The absence of shine adds a touch of understated beauty to your living spaces. Our professional painters bring out the best in your home with Vinilex 5000 paint, delivering a lasting and refined finish.

Matex paint is an excellent economic choice that doesn't compromise quality. It stands out as a great option for painting services for rental properties, providing a reasonable yet presentable solution for wall painting. It ensures that your rental space maintains a clean and inviting look while staying within budget constraints. Prices to avail of painting services differ with the size of your home. Professional Painting Process

The painting process by professional painters at 5 Star Painting involves a series of steps designed to achieve a flawless and polished finish.

01

Complimentary site visit

Our project manager will come down for a site survey to inspect the wall conditions and provide recommendations for paint type and colours

Non-obligatory quote

Customised quotation will be provided after a site survey

02

03

Choosing of colours

Selection and recommendation of paint colours

04

Shifting & protecting your furniture

Furniture will be shifted and protected with professional-grade drop sheets to prevent paint drips. Carpentry and flooring that does not require painting will be protected too.

05

Painting

Residential painting services are done by our in-house professional painters using genuine paint.
It begins with thorough surface preparation, including cleaning and repairing any imperfections.
Applying a primer creates a smooth foundation and enhances paint adhesion.
Once the surface is ready, our professional painters carefully cut in around edges and corners before using rollers or brushes to apply the paint evenly.
Multiple coats may be required to ensure optimal coverage and depth of colour.
Attention to detail is paramount throughout the process, ensuring clean lines, seamless application, and sufficient drying time between coats.
Area that was started will be finished by the end of the day.

06

Reinstating of furniture

Drop sheets will be removed and furniture will be shifted back to the original positions.

07

Handover

The painting contractors will perform final touch-ups A meticulous inspection is conducted to guarantee the desired outcome, with any necessary touch-ups or adjustments made with 1 year warranty given upon completion.

No. Our contractors take necessary precautions to minimise any mess or dirt during the painting process. We use protective covers for furniture and floors, and we clean up the work area after completion, leaving your house in a clean and tidy condition.
