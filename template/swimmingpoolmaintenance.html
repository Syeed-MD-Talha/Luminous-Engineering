SWIMMING POOL MAINTENANCE SERVICES

Keep your pool in perfect condition with our comprehensive maintenance services.

1. Swimming Pool Cleaning Services

Professional pool cleaning services let you enjoy a sparkling, clean, and inviting pool all year long.

Our skilled technicians begin with thorough skimming to clear leaves and other debris from the surface. This essential step enhances the pool’s visual appeal, prevents clogging, and improves water circulation.

Following the skimming, we brush the walls and tiles. This process prevents algae buildup that could lead to slippery surfaces and a murky appearance. Brushing also helps remove potential stains before they set in.

We also clean the waterline and corners of the pool where dirt and oils accumulate. Doing so prevents the formation of scum lines.

The cleaning process concludes with vacuuming the pool floor to remove any settled dirt, maintaining a pristine swimming environment.

Our team uses pool-safe cleaning agents for proper swimming pool sanitation throughout each cleaning session.

2. Water Testing & Chemical Balancing

The right chemical balance in your pool needs to be maintained for the safety and comfort of swimmers.

Our swimming pool water analysis and treatment services include checking and adjusting pH and chlorine levels. These aim to prevent bacteria and algae growth.

We also monitor and ensure the turbidity and Heterotrophic Plate Count are within the safe limits.

Our pool maintenance specialists are trained to handle water sampling and analysis. They ensure your pool water chemistry is well-balanced.

3. Filtration System Maintenance

A functioning filtration system ensures crystal clear water and a healthy swimming environment.

Our maintenance program includes cleaning and backwashing filters, suitable for any type including sand filters.

We monitor the filtration system’s efficiency by checking the pressure differential and flow rate.

Regular maintenance of the filtration system prevents common problems, and also helps to extend the lifespan of your pool equipment.

4. Pump & Equipment Maintenance

Pumps, motors, and other swimming pool equipment need to be maintained to ensure your pool operates smoothly.

Our technicians are trained to service all types of pool equipment, identifying and rectifying any potential malfunctions before they become major issues.

Regular inspections help prevent leaks and other common equipment failures, ensuring your pool’s mechanical systems are always in top condition.

5. Pool Automation System Maintenance

Many pools today are equipped with automated systems such as automated chlorinators and other smart devices that enhance pool management.

Our Pool Automation System Maintenance service ensures these systems are operating correctly, with regular updates and checks to software and hardware.

This maintenance is crucial for keeping your pool’s automation features functional and efficient, significantly easing the management and upkeep of your pool.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Swimming Pool Repair in Singapore

What are common signs that my pool needs repair?
  • Water loss more than usual (leaks)
  • Discolored or cloudy water
  • Visible cracks or damage in pool structure
  • Equipment malfunctioning or making unusual noises
  • Algae growth despite regular cleaning
How can I tell if my pool has a leak?
  • Unexplained decrease in water level
  • Wet spots around the pool area
  • Increasing water bills without extra usage
  • Air bubbles in the pool system
How often should I have my pool inspected?

Have your pool professionally inspected at least once a year. If your pool is heavily used, consider more frequent inspections.
