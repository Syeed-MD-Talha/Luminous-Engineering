HANDYMAN SERVICES IN SINGAPORE

Luminous Engineering provides comprehensive handyman services across Singapore for homeowners, landlords, and businesses. Our skilled handymen are equipped to handle a wide variety of home maintenance, repair, and installation tasks—saving you time, effort, and the hassle of coordinating multiple contractors.

Whether you need to fix a leaky faucet, install shelving, mount your TV, assemble furniture, patch walls, or tackle any other household task, our experienced team delivers reliable solutions. We bring the right tools, expertise, and professional approach to every job, no matter how big or small.

Our handyman services are perfect for those odd jobs that don't require a specialized contractor but need professional attention. We pride ourselves on punctuality, clear communication, and quality workmanship. Many of our clients use our services regularly for ongoing maintenance of their properties.

With Luminous Engineering, you get honest pricing, efficient service, and the peace of mind that comes from working with insured, experienced professionals. We're your one-call solution for keeping your home or office in perfect working order.

1. General Home Repairs

Our handymen fix common household issues quickly and professionally. From door repairs to window fixes, we handle it all.

Services include fixing squeaky or sticking doors, repairing damaged window frames and screens, patching holes in walls and ceilings, replacing damaged tiles, fixing loose cabinet handles and hinges, and general carpentry repairs around your home.

We also help landlords prepare units for handover with quick cosmetic fixes, ensure safety issues are addressed before inspections, and photograph completed work for your records.

  • On-the-spot carpentry touch-ups using matching fillers and laminates.
  • Weatherproofing for windows and balcony doors to keep rainwater out.
  • Preventive maintenance checklists so recurring faults are tackled early.

We diagnose problems accurately and provide lasting solutions that prevent issues from recurring. Our goal is to keep your home in excellent condition.

2. Installation Services

Professional installation of various fixtures, fittings, and equipment throughout your home or office.

We install curtain rods and blinds, shelving and storage systems, TV wall mounts and brackets, mirrors and picture frames, door locks and handles, towel bars and bathroom accessories, kitchen and bathroom fixtures, and ceiling fans. Every installation is done with precision and care to ensure safety and proper function.

  • Anchors selected for the exact wall type (drywall, concrete, hollow block).
  • Laser levelling keeps long runs of shelves or rods perfectly straight.
  • Post-install cleanup so the area is dust-free and ready to use immediately.

Need multiple fittings installed in one visit? We map out the order of work to minimise noise and disruption to your household.

3. Furniture Assembly

Skip the frustration of assembling flat-pack furniture. Our handymen expertly assemble all types of furniture quickly and correctly.

We assemble wardrobes, beds, office desks and chairs, dining tables, bookshelves, TV consoles, outdoor furniture, and children's furniture. We follow manufacturer instructions precisely, ensure all parts are properly secured, and clean up all packaging materials. Your furniture will be sturdy, stable, and ready to use.

  • Missing screws or dowels? We'll supply equivalents on the spot whenever possible.
  • Optional anti-tip brackets available for tall cabinets and nursery pieces.
  • Reconfiguration help for modular furniture when you move or renovate.

Need disposal for old furniture? Let us know in advance and we can coordinate haul-away services.

4. Painting & Touch-Up Services

Refresh your space with our painting services. We handle single rooms, feature walls, and touch-up work.

Services include painting individual rooms, creating accent or feature walls, touching up scuffs and marks, filling and painting over holes and cracks, painting doors and window frames, and repainting cabinets and furniture. We use quality paints, proper preparation techniques, and clean work practices to deliver professional results.

  • Colour-matching service for older walls so touch-ups blend seamlessly.
  • Protective coverings for floors, fixtures, and furniture to avoid splatter.
  • Low-odor paint options ideal for nurseries, clinics, or sensitive occupants.

We can also coordinate larger repainting projects with our painting division if you decide to extend the scope.

5. Minor Plumbing & Electrical Work

Handle small plumbing and electrical tasks safely and efficiently. For major work, we coordinate with licensed specialists.

Plumbing services include fixing dripping taps, unclogging sinks and drains, replacing washers and seals, installing new taps and showerheads, and connecting washing machines and dishwashers. Electrical services include replacing light bulbs and fixtures, installing switches and sockets (with licensed electrician when required), troubleshooting minor issues, and coordinating major electrical work. We prioritize safety and code compliance in all work.

  • Waterproof testing after every plumbing repair to ensure zero seepage.
  • Voltage and polarity checks for new electrical points before handover.
  • Direct escalation path to our licensed plumbing/electrical teams for complex cases.

This hybrid service keeps smaller issues from escalating while giving you direct access to specialists when the scope expands.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Handyman Services

What's the minimum service time?

Our minimum service time is 2 hours for most handyman jobs. This covers travel time, tool setup, and ensures we can complete your task properly. For larger projects, we can provide accurate time estimates and fixed quotes during our initial consultation.

Do you provide the tools and materials?

Yes, our handymen come fully equipped with common tools needed for most jobs. For specific materials like fixtures, tiles, paint, or hardware, we can either purchase them on your behalf (with prior approval) or you can provide them. We'll advise you in advance about any materials needed.

How quickly can you come?
  • Same-day service available for urgent repairs
  • Next-day service for most standard requests
  • Scheduled appointments for specific dates and times
  • Emergency service available 24/7 (premium rates apply)
  • We'll confirm availability when you contact us
What areas do you cover?

We provide handyman services islandwide across Singapore including all HDB estates, condominiums, landed properties, and commercial premises. Some remote areas may incur additional travel charges, which we'll inform you about upfront.

Are your handymen insured and trained?

Yes, all our handymen are experienced professionals covered by our company insurance. They undergo regular training and are skilled in various trades. For specialized work requiring licenses (like major electrical work), we coordinate with certified professionals.

Can you handle multiple tasks in one visit?

Absolutely! This is one of the main benefits of our handyman service. You can create a list of tasks, and we'll work through them efficiently during our visit. This saves you time and money compared to scheduling multiple specialist contractors for different small jobs.

What if I'm not satisfied with the work?

Your satisfaction is our priority. If you're not happy with any work, inform us immediately and we'll rectify the issue at no extra charge. We stand behind our workmanship and want every customer to be completely satisfied with our service.

Do you offer regular maintenance packages?

Yes, we offer monthly or quarterly maintenance packages for homeowners, landlords, and businesses who need regular upkeep. These packages provide priority scheduling, discounted rates, and proactive maintenance to prevent costly repairs. Contact us for custom package details.
