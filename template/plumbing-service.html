1. Leak Detection & Repair

Fast and accurate leak detection and repair services to prevent water damage and reduce water bills.

Our experienced plumbers use advanced equipment to locate hidden leaks in pipes, taps, and fixtures.

We perform pressure testing, moisture mapping, and borescope inspections so we can pinpoint the source of the leak without unnecessary hacking.

  • Thermal imaging detects concealed leaks behind tiles and cabinetry.
  • Pipe tracing identifies hairline cracks along both copper and UPVC runs.
  • Remedial work includes re-sealing joints, replacing faulty fittings, and reinforcing weakened sections.

Timely leak repair prevents water damage, reduces wastage, and saves on utility costs.

2. Pipe Installation & Replacement

Professional pipe installation and replacement services using quality materials that last.

All pipe routes are planned to comply with PUB standards and to keep future access panels convenient.

  • Material options include stainless steel, copper, PEX, and UPVC depending on usage and budget.
  • Noise and vibration control achieved through proper bracketing and insulation sleeves.
  • Pressure balancing ensures consistent flow between bathrooms, kitchens, and outdoor taps.

We replace old, corroded pipes with modern materials that provide better water flow, prevent leaks, and ensure clean water supply.

3. Drain Cleaning & Unclogging

Effective drain cleaning services to clear blockages and restore proper water flow.

We assess the extent of buildup with inspection cameras before recommending high-pressure jetting, manual augers, or enzyme treatment.

  • Kitchen grease traps degreased to stop fats from hardening further down the line.
  • Toilet and shower traps descaled to remove soap scum, hair, and foreign objects.
  • Commercial floor drains scheduled on preventive maintenance plans to avoid downtime.

Our plumbers use professional equipment to clear stubborn clogs, ensuring fast drainage, preventing backups, and eliminating odors.

4. Water Heater Installation & Repair

Complete water heater services including installation, repair, and replacement.

We check wiring, pressure relief valves, and plumbing connections so every heater is commissioned safely.

  • Descaling extends the lifespan of storage tanks and improves heating efficiency.
  • Thermostat calibration keeps water temperatures consistent across all bathrooms.
  • Mounting solutions cover HDB-approved instant heaters to heavy-duty multipoint systems.

We service all types of water heaters including instant heaters and storage tanks, ensuring hot water availability, energy efficiency, and safe operation.

5. Bathroom & Kitchen Fixture Installation

Professional installation of taps, sinks, toilets, showers, and kitchen fixtures.

We coordinate with interior designers and carpenters so countertop cut-outs, tile drilling, and plumbing points align perfectly.

  • Water-saving fittings supplied with PUB-approved ratings.
  • Smart bidet seats, rain showers, and mixer taps installed with proper pressure balancing valves.
  • Under-sink filtration, garbage disposals, and dishwasher hookups configured with anti-siphon protection.

We install high-quality fixtures with proper connections that ensure no leaks, smooth operation, and enhanced aesthetics.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Plumbing Services in Singapore

How quickly can you respond to plumbing emergencies?

We offer 24/7 emergency plumbing services with response times within 2 hours during office hours and within 4 hours after office hours. For urgent leaks or burst pipes, we prioritize immediate response.

What are common signs of plumbing problems?
  • Dripping taps or visible leaks
  • Slow draining sinks or showers
  • Low water pressure
  • Running toilets or constant refilling
  • Water stains on walls or ceilings
  • Unpleasant odors from drains
Do you provide warranty for plumbing work?

Yes, we provide warranty coverage for all plumbing installations and repairs. The warranty period typically ranges from 6 months to 1 year depending on the service type and materials used.

Can you help with HDB plumbing issues?

Absolutely! We have extensive experience with HDB plumbing systems and understand the specific requirements. We handle all types of HDB plumbing work including repairs, installations, and renovations.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept various payment methods including cash, bank transfer, PayNow, and credit cards for your convenience. Payment terms will be discussed during quotation.
