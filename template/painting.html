FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Painting Services in Singapore

How long does a typical painting project take?

The duration depends on the size and complexity of the project. A standard 3-room HDB flat typically takes 3-5 days, while a 5-room flat may require 5-7 days. Condos and larger properties may take 7-10 days. We provide a detailed timeline during the quotation process.

What brands of paint do you use?

We use premium quality paints from trusted brands including Nippon Paint, Dulux, and Jotun. Our packages feature different paint grades - Matex for basic coverage, Vinilex 5000 for enhanced durability, and Odour-less All-in-1 for premium odorless finishes.

Do I need to move out during the painting work?

It's not mandatory to move out. However, we recommend staying elsewhere if possible, especially during the first 2-3 days when the painting smell is strongest. If you choose to stay, we use low-odor paints and ensure proper ventilation. Our premium package uses odorless paint for minimal disruption.

Are there any hidden costs in your quotations?

No, we believe in complete transparency. Our quotations include all costs - labor, materials, surface preparation, and cleanup. Any additional work required will be discussed and approved by you before proceeding. What you see in the quote is what you pay.

Do you provide warranty for your painting work?

Yes, we provide a 1-year warranty for all our painting services. This covers peeling, bubbling, or any defects in workmanship. The warranty does not cover damage caused by external factors like water leakage or physical impact.

What preparation work do you do before painting?

We thoroughly prepare all surfaces by scraping off old loose paint, filling cracks and holes, sanding rough areas, and applying primer where needed. We also protect your furniture and flooring with protective covers. Proper preparation ensures the paint adheres well and lasts longer.

Can I choose custom colors for my walls?

Absolutely! We offer free color consultation and you can choose from thousands of colors available in our paint catalogs. Our team can help you select colors that complement your interior design and lighting. We can also provide color samples for you to test on your walls.

Do you clean up after the painting work?

Yes, thorough cleanup is included in all our packages. After completing the painting work, we remove all protective coverings, clean paint splatters, dispose of waste materials properly, and leave your home clean and ready to use. We ensure minimal disruption to your daily life.

What is the difference between your Basic, Enhanced, and Premium packages?

Our Basic Package uses Nippon Paint Matex for affordable, quality coverage. The Enhanced Package features Vinilex 5000 with superior durability and washability - our most popular choice. The Premium Package uses Odour-less All-in-1 paint that's completely odorless and includes additional features like anti-bacterial properties and faster drying time.

How soon can you start work after confirmation?

Subject to our schedule, we can typically start within 3-7 days after quotation approval and deposit payment. For urgent projects, we may be able to accommodate earlier start dates. We recommend booking in advance, especially during peak renovation periods.
