OUR STORY

Building Dreams, Creating Legacies

Founded in 2015, Luminous Engineering began with a simple vision: to bring world-class home renovation and handyman services to Singapore. What started as a small team of passionate craftsmen has grown into one of the most trusted names in the industry.

Our journey has been marked by an unwavering commitment to excellence, transparency, and customer satisfaction. Every project we undertake is a testament to our dedication to quality and our passion for transforming spaces into something extraordinary.

Today, we pride ourselves on being more than just a service provider. We're partners in bringing your vision to life, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern techniques to deliver results that exceed expectations.
