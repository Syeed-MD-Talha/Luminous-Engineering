FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Home Renovation in Singapore

How long does a full home renovation take?

A typical full home renovation takes 8-12 weeks depending on the size and complexity. Smaller HDB flats may take 6-8 weeks, while larger condominiums or landed properties can take 12-20 weeks. We provide a detailed timeline during the quotation stage and keep you updated throughout the project.

Do I need to move out during renovation?

For full home renovations, we strongly recommend moving out for your comfort and safety. However, for partial renovations (single rooms), you may be able to stay with proper dust barriers and scheduling. We'll discuss the best approach based on your specific project scope.

Do you handle renovation permits and approvals?
  • Yes, we handle all necessary permits and approvals
  • HDB renovation permits (if required)
  • MCST approvals for condominiums
  • Building plan submissions for structural changes
  • Coordination with building management
What's included in your renovation package?

Our packages typically include design consultation, hacking and demolition, disposal, masonry work, plumbing, electrical wiring, carpentry, tiling, painting, and installation of fixtures. Specific inclusions depend on your chosen package and customization. We provide detailed quotations listing all items.

Can I customize the renovation design?

Absolutely! We encourage customization. Our design team works closely with you to understand your vision, lifestyle needs, and budget. We provide 3D visualizations and material samples to help you make informed decisions. Every renovation is tailored to your preferences.

What is your payment schedule?

We typically follow a progressive payment schedule: 10% upon signing contract, 30% after hacking/demolition, 30% at mid-stage completion, 25% upon practical completion, and 5% after defects rectification. This protects both parties and ensures quality work at every stage.

Do you provide warranty for your work?

Yes, we provide a comprehensive 1-year warranty on workmanship for all our renovation projects. Specific fixtures and materials may have longer manufacturer warranties. We also offer responsive after-sales service to address any issues that may arise.

How do you ensure quality control?

Every project has a dedicated project manager who conducts regular site inspections, coordinates all trades, and ensures work meets our quality standards. We use experienced contractors, quality materials, and follow industry best practices. Clients receive regular photo updates and can schedule site visits anytime.
