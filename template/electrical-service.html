1. Electrical Installation

Professional installation of electrical systems, wiring, and fixtures for new constructions and renovations.

Our licensed electricians install lighting systems, power outlets, circuit breakers, and complete electrical distribution boards.

We handle load calculations, recommend energy-efficient layouts, and coordinate with ID contractors so every point is properly positioned before tiling or carpentry works begin.

  • 3D point-marking plans help owners visualise socket and lighting locations.
  • Concealed trunking and EMT conduit used to keep interiors neat while remaining code-compliant.
  • All hardware sourced from EMA-approved suppliers with documented warranties.

Proper installation ensures safety, prevents electrical hazards, and meets Singapore's electrical codes.

2. Electrical Repair & Troubleshooting

Fast and reliable repairs for electrical faults, tripped breakers, and power outages.

We run insulation resistance tests, breaker load checks, and thermal scans so we fix the root cause instead of just the symptoms.

  • Isolated circuits before repair to keep the rest of your property powered.
  • Emergency parts stocked in our vans to replace switches, sockets, relays, or wiring immediately.
  • Detailed service reports outline the issue, rectification steps, and preventive tips for owners.

Our experienced electricians quickly diagnose and fix electrical problems, ensuring safe operation, restored power, and peace of mind.

3. Lighting Installation & Upgrade

Transform your space with professional lighting solutions including LED upgrades and smart lighting systems.

We help homeowners select the right colour temperature, beam angle, and CRI so living rooms, workspaces, and landscape areas receive optimal illumination.

  • Smart lighting ecosystems integrated with Google Home, Alexa, and Apple HomeKit.
  • Outdoor fixtures sealed to IP65/IP67 ratings for pool decks, balconies, and façades.
  • Accent lighting plans for feature walls, cove ceilings, and display cabinetry.

We install indoor and outdoor lighting that enhances ambiance, improves energy efficiency, and increases property value.

4. Complete Rewiring Services

Upgrade old or faulty wiring to meet modern electrical demands and safety standards.

We check insulation breakdown, aluminium-to-copper joints, and ageing junction boxes before designing a phased rewiring plan.

  • New circuits labelled clearly within the distribution board for easier maintenance.
  • Surface or concealed routing selected based on the renovation stage and budget.
  • Power quality tests confirm the rewired system meets current load requirements.

Our rewiring services eliminate fire hazards, support modern appliances, and comply with regulations.

5. Distribution Board (DB Box) Installation

Professional installation and upgrade of electrical distribution boards for safe power distribution.

We assess existing load layouts, recommend RCBO/MCB combinations, and include surge protection where sensitive electronics are present.

  • Clean wiring management with ferrules and trunking for easy future servicing.
  • Main switch upgrades sized to accommodate EV chargers, induction hobs, or large HVAC systems.
  • Testing and commissioning reports issued in line with EMA and SCDF guidelines.

We install modern DB boxes with proper circuit protection that prevents overloads, organizes circuits, and ensures compliance with safety standards.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Electrical Services in Singapore

Are your electricians licensed?

Yes, all our electricians are fully licensed and registered with the Energy Market Authority (EMA) of Singapore. They are qualified to perform all types of electrical work safely and legally.

What are signs that I need electrical repairs?
  • Frequently tripping circuit breakers
  • Flickering or dimming lights
  • Burning smell from outlets or switches
  • Warm or discolored outlets
  • Sparks when plugging in appliances
Do you provide emergency electrical services?

Yes, we offer 24/7 emergency electrical services. Our electricians can respond within 2-4 hours depending on the time of day. Call us immediately for electrical emergencies.

Do you provide warranty for electrical work?

Yes, we provide warranty coverage for all electrical work performed. The warranty period varies depending on the service type, typically ranging from 6 months to 2 years for labor and materials.

Can you help with upgrading old electrical systems?

Absolutely! We specialize in upgrading old electrical systems including rewiring, DB box upgrades, and modernizing electrical installations to meet current safety standards and support modern electrical loads.
