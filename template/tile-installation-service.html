PROFESSIONAL TILE INSTALLATION SERVICES IN SINGAPORE

Luminous Engineering provides expert floor and wall tile installation services across Singapore. Our skilled tilers bring years of experience in working with ceramic, porcelain, marble, granite, mosaic, and various specialty tiles to create stunning, durable surfaces.

Whether you're renovating a bathroom, upgrading your kitchen, or tiling an entire home or commercial space, our team ensures precise alignment, consistent grout lines, and a flawless finish. We understand that proper tile installation requires meticulous surface preparation, precise cutting, and attention to detail—skills our craftsmen have perfected over years of practice.

Our tile installation services cover both residential and commercial projects. We work with all tile types and sizes, from small mosaic patterns to large format tiles. Our expertise includes waterproofing for wet areas, proper substrate preparation, expansion joint installation, and using high-quality adhesives and grouts that ensure longevity.

At Luminous Engineering, we're committed to delivering tile installations that not only look beautiful but also stand the test of time. Our competitive pricing, professional service, and quality workmanship have made us a trusted choice for homeowners and businesses across Singapore.

1. Tile Interior Design

We're fully prepared to understand the tiling aesthetic you are going for, and would gladly make professional recommendations regarding tile material and tile placement.

HDB Approved Tiler with Tile Interior Design Assessment and Consultation. Our design services help you visualize the perfect tile layout for your space, ensuring both functionality and aesthetics are considered in every project.

We provide cement screeding for a smooth, even and sturdy cement screed base guaranteed for unobstructed and hassle-free tile or vinyl layering.

HDB Approved tile & floor contractor in Singapore with cement screed services for smooth application. Proper screeding is essential for a long-lasting tile installation that won't crack or lift over time.

3. Floor and Wall Tile Installation

We provide a wide range of customizable options for tiles including material and colour, all guaranteed to meet and exceed expectations.

HDB Approved direct tile contractor specializing in bathroom, toilet, wall & floor tile installation. Our skilled craftsmen ensure precise alignment, consistent grout lines, and a flawless finish that transforms your space.

4. Waterproofing Services

We are aware of the unpleasantries and inconveniences caused by leaks and are fully committed to preventing water leaks with quality waterproofing works.

HDB Approved direct tile contractor with comprehensive waterproofing services for wet areas in HDB homes including kitchens & bathrooms. Proper waterproofing ensures your tiles remain secure and your home stays protected from water damage.

5. Floor and Wall Tile Overlay

We provide on-site consultation services to assess your floor and/or wall tiles and ensure that your existing tiles are suitable for an overlay finish without hacking.

HDB Approved direct tilers offering overlay tiles installation services. This cost-effective solution saves you time and money by installing new tiles directly over existing ones, eliminating the need for messy tile removal.

6. Construct Wall Services

We provide professional and affordable construct wall services according to HDB safety and standard guidelines.

HDB Approved floor and wall tile contractor offering partition wall construction for BTO and resale HDB homes. Our construct wall services comply with all HDB regulations, ensuring safe and approved modifications to your living space.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does tile installation take?

The duration depends on the area size and complexity. A standard bathroom typically takes 3-5 days, while a living room might take 5-7 days. We will provide a detailed timeline during the consultation.

Do you provide warranty for tiling works?

Yes, we provide a warranty for our workmanship. This covers any installation defects such as loose tiles or hollow sounds. The specific warranty period will be stated in our quotation.

Can you tile over existing tiles?

Yes, overlaying is possible if the existing tiles are sound and the floor level permits. This is a cost-effective and faster option compared to hacking. We will assess your site to determine if overlaying is suitable.
