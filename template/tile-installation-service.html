FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Tile Installation in Singapore

How long does tile installation take?

Installation time depends on the area size and complexity. A typical bathroom (5-7 sqm) takes 2-3 days, while a complete home floor tiling project might take 1-2 weeks. This includes surface preparation, tile laying, grouting, and curing time. We provide detailed timelines during quotation.

What tiles are best for Singapore's climate?
  • Porcelain tiles - low water absorption, durable
  • Ceramic tiles - cost-effective, moisture resistant
  • Homogeneous tiles - excellent for high-traffic areas
  • Anti-slip tiles - essential for wet areas
  • Avoid natural stone in very humid areas unless sealed properly
Is waterproofing necessary before tiling?

Yes, waterproofing is essential for all wet areas including bathrooms, kitchens, balconies, and utility areas. We apply waterproofing membranes to prevent water seepage that can damage the structure and cause tiles to loosen. This is a critical step that shouldn't be skipped.

Can you match existing tiles for repairs?

We do our best to match existing tiles for repair work. If the exact tile is discontinued, we'll source the closest match possible. For visible areas, we may recommend replacing a larger section to ensure uniformity. We always show samples before proceeding with repairs.

How do I maintain my tiled surfaces?

Regular maintenance includes sweeping or vacuuming to remove dirt, mopping with pH-neutral cleaners, avoiding harsh chemicals that can damage grout, and resealing grout lines every 1-2 years for longevity. For polished tiles, avoid abrasive cleaners that can dull the finish.

What's the difference between ceramic and porcelain tiles?

Porcelain tiles are denser, less porous, and more durable than ceramic tiles. They're better for high-traffic areas and outdoor use. Ceramic tiles are more affordable and sufficient for wall applications and low-traffic areas. Both are suitable for Singapore's climate when properly installed.

Do you remove old tiles before installing new ones?

Yes, we recommend removing old tiles to ensure a proper foundation. This allows us to check for structural issues, apply fresh waterproofing, ensure proper leveling, and achieve a professional finish. In some cases, tiles can be laid over existing tiles, but this is only advisable in specific situations.

What warranty do you provide?

We provide a 1-year workmanship warranty covering issues like tile delamination, hollow tiles, or grout failure due to improper installation. Tile materials have separate manufacturer warranties. We also offer after-sales service for any concerns that arise after project completion.
