HACKING & DEMOLITION IN SINGAPORE

Luminous Engineering provides professional hacking and demolition services for residential and commercial properties in Singapore. Whether you are renovating your kitchen, bathroom, or doing a complete overhaul of your HDB or condo, our experienced team ensures safe and compliant demolition works.

Hacking is the first and most crucial step in any renovation project. It involves the removal of existing tiles, walls, built-in cabinets, and sanitary wares. Our team is well-versed in HDB regulations and safety protocols to ensure that structural integrity is maintained while preparing your space for its new look.

We handle the entire process, from obtaining necessary permits to the actual hacking work and debris disposal. Our efficient workflow minimizes noise and dust disruption, and we ensure that all debris is properly disposed of at approved dumping grounds.

1. Wall Hacking & Demolition

Open up your living space by removing non-structural walls. We carefully assess and hack walls to create open-concept kitchens or larger living areas.

Our team ensures that all electrical wiring and concealed pipes are safely terminated or diverted before hacking begins. We strictly follow HDB and BCA guidelines to prevent any damage to structural components.

2. Floor & Wall Tile Hacking

Removing old, worn-out tiles is essential for a fresh start. We provide hacking services for floor and wall tiles in kitchens, bathrooms, living rooms, and bedrooms.

Our process includes thorough removal of tiles and cement screed, leaving a clean surface ready for waterproofing and new tiling works. We use proper machinery to ensure efficiency and precision.

3. Built-in Cabinet Dismantling

We dismantle and dispose of old kitchen cabinets, wardrobes, TV consoles, and other built-in furniture.

Our team carefully dismantles carpentry to avoid damage to surrounding walls and floors that are to be retained. We handle the heavy lifting and disposal, so you don't have to worry about the mess.

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions about Hacking Services

Do I need a permit for hacking?

Yes, for HDB flats, a renovation permit is required before any hacking works can commence. For condominiums, approval from the MCST is needed. We assist in applying for all necessary permits.

How long does hacking take?

Hacking works typically take between 3 to 5 days for a standard HDB flat, depending on the extent of the demolition. We adhere to the approved renovation hours to minimize disturbance to neighbors.

Is debris removal included?

Yes, our hacking packages include the clearing and disposal of all renovation debris. We ensure that debris is transported to approved dumping grounds.

Can you hack structural walls?

No, structural walls (load-bearing walls) cannot be hacked as it affects the building's stability. We will review the floor plan and advise on which walls can be safely removed.
