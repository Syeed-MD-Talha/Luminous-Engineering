Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our services and process.

How quickly can you start my project?

Project start times vary based on scope and current schedule. Small projects like painting can often begin within 1-2 weeks, while larger renovations may require 3-4 weeks lead time. Emergency services are available 24/7.

Do you provide warranties?

Yes! All our work comes with comprehensive warranties. Painting services include 2-year warranty, electrical work has 1-year warranty, and renovation projects come with 5-year structural warranty. Terms and conditions apply.

Are you licensed and insured?

Absolutely. We are HDB licensed, EMA certified for electrical work, PUB licensed for plumbing, and carry comprehensive insurance coverage. All our team members are certified professionals with years of experience.

Can you help with design and permits?

Yes, we provide complete design consultation services and handle all necessary permits for your renovation. Our team includes certified interior designers and we manage the entire process from concept to completion.

What areas do you service?

We service all areas of Singapore including HDB flats, condominiums, landed properties, and commercial spaces. Our team is mobile and can reach any location on the island within 24 hours for urgent requests.
