About Luminous Engineering

For over 8 years, we've been transforming homes across Singapore with premium craftsmanship, innovative solutions, and unwavering commitment to excellence.

Our Story

Founded in 2015 by a team of experienced craftsmen and designers, Luminous Engineering was born from a simple vision: to bring premium home renovation services to Singapore homeowners with transparency, reliability, and exceptional quality.

What started as a small team of dedicated professionals has grown into Singapore's most trusted renovation partner, completing over 500 projects and maintaining a 98% customer satisfaction rate. Our commitment to excellence has earned us recognition as industry leaders in premium home transformation.

Today, we continue to innovate and set new standards in the renovation industry, combining traditional craftsmanship with modern technology to deliver results that exceed expectations and enhance the value of every home we touch.
